Privacy Notice (Nigeria NDPA 2023)

Data Controller: Apparel by Ezekiel Atelier (Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria).

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, we are committed to transparent, lawful handling of your personal data.

1. What We Collect

  • Contact details: Customer Name, WhatsApp phone number, and optional email.
  • Bespoke information: Body measurements, fitting appointment dates, and styling preferences.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To schedule, coordinate, and confirm bespoke garment fittings.
  • To notify subscribed clients of limited textile arrivals and seasonal drops.

3. Zero-Upload Virtual Try-On

Our Virtual Try-On technology executes entirely in your client browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers, third parties, or cloud storage.

4. Your Rights

Under the NDPA 2023, you retain the right to inspect, rectify, or request immediate deletion of your contact records by contacting Ezekiel Concierge via WhatsApp or atelier email.
